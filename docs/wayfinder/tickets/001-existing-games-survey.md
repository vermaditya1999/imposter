## Question

What do existing imposter / Undercover / "Who is the spy" games actually ship as word data, and what do players complain about? Gather concrete examples (bad pairs, bad categories, repetition, translation issues, culturally off references) from app-store reviews, Reddit/forums and any published word lists, mapped onto the six quality-bar points in the map's Notes. Output feeds the Quality Checklist.

## Resolution

The loudest complaint is repetition and running out of words (top Undercover app gives 50 free words); ~400 pairs is competitive if no pair repeats until the pool is exhausted. Published lists show duplicate words, inconsistent difficulty and misfiled categories; translations are a known weak spot. Copy: set difficulty by how close the two words are (Sun/Moon easy, Dog/Wolf medium, Frog/Toad hard), not by rarity. Evidence is thinner than ideal (Reddit unreachable).
