## Question

Which 12–15 Categories make up v1, what does each one include and exclude, and do they fit the group (references, places, food, pop culture they actually share)?

## Resolution

*Decided by Claude on the user's delegation (2026-10-09). This is the decision most likely to need the user's input, because only they know the group. Each Category is cheap to swap until its Pairs are authored.*

14 Categories × ~30 Pairs ≈ 420 Pairs. Each one is a single kind of thing (QC-11) and can supply ~60 unique familiar words (QC-12).

| # | Category | Includes | Excludes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Indian Food | Savoury dishes, street food, snacks, breads, accompaniments and Indo-Chinese (Samosa/Kachori, Gobi Manchurian/Chilli Paneer) *(Indo-Chinese and accompaniments added by the pilot)* | Sweets (→ 2), dishes that are only regional |
| 2 | Sweets & Desserts | Mithai and desserts, Indian and Western (Rasgulla/Rasmalai, Brownie/Cake) | Drinks |
| 3 | World Food | Non-Indian dishes common in Indian cities (Pizza/Burger, Sushi/Dim Sum) | Brands (→ 11) |
| 4 | Animals | Animals, birds, insects, sea creatures (Dog/Wolf, Frog/Toad) | Fictional creatures |
| 5 | Places Around Town | Everyday places and venues (Mall/Market, Gym/Park, Dhaba/Café) | Named places (→ 6), places of worship (QC-06) |
| 6 | Travel Destinations | Cities, hill stations, beaches and countries you'd go to on a trip (Goa/Kerala, Shimla/Manali, Dubai/Singapore) | Politically charged pairings (QC-06) |
| 7 | Jobs | Professions (Doctor/Nurse, Chef/Waiter) | Specific famous people |
| 8 | Sports & Games | Sports, board games, playground and party games (Cricket/Baseball, Carrom/Pool, Ludo/Snakes and Ladders) | Players and teams |
| 9 | Movies & Shows | Titles of long-lived films and series, Bollywood and international (Sholay/DDLJ, Friends/Big Bang Theory) | Actors, anything less than ~5 years old (QC-07) |
| 10 | Characters | Fictional characters: superheroes, cartoons, classic film characters (Batman/Iron Man, Chhota Bheem/Doraemon, Shaktimaan/Krrish) | Real people, religious or mythological figures (QC-06) |
| 11 | Brands & Apps | Consumer brands and apps the group uses (Swiggy/Zomato, Amazon/Flipkart, Maggi/Yippee) | Political or controversial companies |
| 12 | Around the House | Household objects and appliances (Pressure Cooker/Kadai, Fridge/Microwave) | Gadgets (→ 14), clothes (→ 13) |
| 13 | Clothes & Accessories | Clothing, footwear, jewellery, accessories (Kurta/Sherwani, Saree/Lehenga, Sneakers/Loafers) | Brands |
| 14 | Gadgets | Personal tech and electronics (Laptop/Tablet, Earphones/Headphones, Smartwatch/Fitness Band) | Apps (→ 11), home appliances (→ 12) |

**Ruled out:**
- **Festivals**: these are religious (QC-06).
- **Celebrities**: they go stale and can turn controversial (QC-07).
- **Transport, Musical Instruments**: too few familiar words for ~30 Pairs (QC-12).
- **School & College Life**: it mixes places, objects and events (QC-11).

**Pilot Category:** Indian Food. It's the most local Category, so it tests QC-05 hardest, and it should be the easiest to fill.
